Does more agents help or harm agreement?

  • Liu et al., 2026: Deal welfare increases with greater buyer and seller multiplicity, attributed to market liquidity that lets agents find compatible trading partners.
  • Abdelnabi et al., 2024: Adding parties and tightening the feasible-deal ratio sharply degrades agreement success, dropping GPT-4 from 81% to 30% as feasible deals shrink.
  • Nature: Contextual: price-only matching markets ease finding partners, while multi-issue games with veto parties make agreement combinatorially harder. (topic agent-to-agent-negotiation)

Manipulation resistance of frontier AI shopping agents

  • Bansal et al., 2025: Frontier models robustly resist traditional psychological manipulation tactics (authority, social proof, loss aversion) and keep payments to manipulative businesses low.
  • Allouah et al., 2025: Frontier agents remain substantially exploitable through seller-side listing optimization, with modest description edits driving large market-share swings; Affonso models the same exploitability as the engine of vertical tacit collusion, and Kumar & Lakkaraju (XT585ED2) realize it as an adversarial strategic-text-sequence attack that forces a target product to the top.
  • Nature: Different attack surfaces: conversational psychological persuasion (resisted) versus structural and adversarial exploitation of position, endorsement, and description channels (still effective). (topic ai-mediated-market-dynamics)

Simplified vs. ambiguous benchmark policies

  • Yao et al., 2024: τ-bench and τ²-bench evaluate agents against purpose-built, unambiguous domain policies and treat inconsistent outcomes as agent unreliability measured by pass^k.
  • Borkakoty et al., 2026: DRIP-R grounds tasks in real ambiguous retail policy and shows frontier models fundamentally disagree on resolutions where no single correct outcome exists, reframing apparent unreliability as irreducible decision ambiguity.
  • Nature: Disagreement over whether realistic policy ambiguity should be designed into the benchmark or held simple, and over what inconsistent agent outcomes actually measure. (topic tool-agent-user-interaction)
