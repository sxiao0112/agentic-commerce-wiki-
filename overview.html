What an agentic purchase can look like

A person can ask an AI agent to shop on their behalf. The agent can then use the functions that a business makes available. The Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) core concepts describe one way to connect these participants. The example below is a UCP-style purchase, not a required path for every agent or store.

Who does what:
  • Platform or agent: acts for a person, organization, or other principal and invokes supported business functions.
  • Business: publishes its profile, processes requests, and usually remains the merchant of record for a purchase.
  • Credential provider: protects sensitive user data and can issue a payment token.
  • Payment service provider: processes the charge for the business.

Some UCP features are optional. A business may offer cart management, fulfillment choices, discounts, identity linking, or consent capture. UCP also supports both human-in-the-loop and fully autonomous agent interactions. The platform can use a service only when the parties support a compatible capability and version.
